# Project log

## 2026-09 — LEGO prototype

- Built a functioning LEGO Technic robot arm in approximately 12 hours.
- Geared down the shoulder.
- Replaced an elbow linear-actuator concept that produced only about 10 degrees of motion with continuous gearing.
- Observed roughly 5–10 mm of backlash associated with the worm-drive arrangement.
- Lifted a 100 g wrench from ground level to vertical.
- Lifted a 28.15 g bottle with the arm extended.
- Found that 100 g at the claw near full extension approached the mechanism's limit.
- Confirmed that placing three motors toward the rear helped counterbalance the arm.
- Confirmed that the four-motor design can fit within one Powered Up hub.
- Chose NEMA 17 and NEMA 14 as the current shoulder and elbow motor direction for the printed arm.
- Created the public GitHub repository and initial project structure.

## Logging format for future tests

Record:

- Date and revision
- Joint or module
- Supply voltage
- Motor and driver
- Gear ratio
- Lever length or arm pose
- Payload
- Speed or step rate
- Peak and steady current
- Temperature and test duration
- Backlash or repeatability
- Failure mode or unusual sound
